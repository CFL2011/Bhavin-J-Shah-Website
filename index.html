About Bhavin J. Shah

Hey, it's Bhavin J. Shah, and I want to share my incredible journey with you. Back in 2010, I started making a positive impact on the lives of around 10-15 people, and that kickstarted the journey of Coach For Life, officially established on September 30, 2011.

Starting small, I went from influencing 10 people to reaching a whopping 7,000 in a single session. Over 12 years, I have touched the lives of more than 200,000 people in India and internationally in Dubai, Bangkok and Hong Kong.

Through "Unleash Your Hidden Potential", I have empowered over 300 business owners through 46 in-person and 10 online sessions. I have also conducted 135 free sessions on "Say Thanks To Parents", enriching the lives of over 65,000 individuals.

About Coach For Life

In 2011, Mr. Bhavin J. Shah founded Coach For Life to help people become their best selves. In 2014, CFL launched its first paid workshop, "Unleash Your Hidden Potential", which became a hit with 46 in-person sessions and 10 online sessions.

As CFL expanded, it moved to a bigger office with two floors in 2016. In 2017, a significant live event drew over 7,000 attendees, transforming lives despite some people having to stay outside due to the crowd.

Facing the challenges of 2020, CFL adapted by using online platforms to connect globally. By January 2022, Coach For Life achieved 50,000 YouTube subscribers, and in 2023 celebrated reaching 100,000 subscribers.
